Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Terms of Service  |  日本語

This Privacy Policy explains how PINLOG (the “App”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information. The App is operated by an individual developer based in Japan (“we”, “us”, “our”). We handle personal information in line with Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information and, where they apply to you, the privacy laws of the place where you live, including those described in Sections 11 and 12.

This English Policy applies to users who live outside Japan. If you live in Japan, the Japanese Privacy Policy (プライバシーポリシー) applies instead.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address: when you create an account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose “Hide My Email”, we receive Apple’s relay address instead.
  • Sign in with Apple identifier: if you sign in with Apple, the user identifier Apple gives us, so that we can link your Apple ID to your account.
  • Password (older accounts only): never stored in plain text. We store only a one-way hash (bcrypt) that cannot be turned back into the password. New accounts sign in with a one-time code sent by email and have no password.
  • Display name: set by you the first time you sign in.
  • User ID: assigned automatically when you create an account; you can change it.
  • Gender and year and month of birth (optional): only if you enter them. We do not collect the day of birth.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you upload one.
  • Log photos: photos you upload when you post a Log (up to 4 per Log).
  • Log details: the description, category and place name you enter or choose.
  • Comments: text you write on other users’ Logs.
  • Reports: the reason you choose and any optional details you add when you report content.
  • Messages to us: what you send when you contact us.

If you buy Premium

  • Purchase information: from the App Store, the transaction number, product, expiry date and status (active, in billing grace period, expired, refunded, etc.), and whether the purchase was made in the production or test environment. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple; we never receive them.

Information collected from your device

  • Precise location, only if you allow it:
    • When you post a Log, we use your current location and save it as the Log’s coordinates.
    • When you open the map or search, the App sends your current location to our server so that nearby Logs are shown first. This location is used only for that request; it is not saved or written to our logs.
    • When you search for nearby shops and places to add a Log, the coordinates are matched only against our own place database (based on Overture Maps). They are not sent to any outside map service.
    • If you turn on “Nearby picks” notifications, the App uses your location in the background on your device only; it is not sent to our server (Section 6).
  • Push notification token and App language: if you allow notifications, the token needed to deliver notifications to your device, and the language of the App on that device so that notifications are written in that language.
  • Crash and error information: if the App crashes or an error occurs, the error, where it happened, your device’s OS version and the App version, so that we can find and fix problems. This is processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.). We do not send anything that identifies you (email address, IP address, display name or user ID), and location coordinates are removed.

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. How we use your information

  • Email address: to create your account, sign you in (including sending one-time codes), and send you notices about the App
  • Sign in with Apple identifier / password hash: to confirm it is you when you sign in
  • Display name, User ID and profile photo: to show other users who you are, and to let them find you and check it is you before sending a follow request
  • Gender and year and month of birth: to choose your initial avatar, and to understand our users and improve recommendations (never shown to other users)
  • Log photos and details: to post your Logs, show them on the map to your followers, and let you search and filter Logs
  • Comments: to show your comments on Logs
  • Reports: to deal with reports and prevent misuse
  • Location: to save the coordinates of your Logs, to suggest nearby places when you post, to show nearby Logs first, and for “Nearby picks” notifications (on your device only)
  • Purchase information: to check whether you can use Premium, restore purchases, prevent a purchase being used on other accounts, and answer your questions
  • Push token and App language: to send you notifications in your language
  • Crash and error information: to detect and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Logs are never public. A Log is shown only to people whose follow request you have approved (your followers). Logs set to “Only me” are shown only to you.
  • A comment is shown to everyone who can see that Log. It is not a private conversation between you and the poster.
  • Photos are served only through signed URLs that expire, never through public URLs.
  • Nothing you post is indexed by search engines.
  • Your display name and User ID can be searched and seen by other users of the App. Your gender and year and month of birth are never shown to other users.

4. Where we store your information

  • Servers: a database on Amazon Web Services (AWS) EC2 in the Tokyo region (ap-northeast-1), Japan
  • Photos: Amazon S3 in the Tokyo region, encrypted at rest
  • Backups: a daily copy of the database is saved to Amazon S3 in the Tokyo region and deleted after 7 days

International transfers. Because we are based in Japan, your information is stored and processed in Japan. The service providers listed in Section 5 may also process it in the United States and other countries where they operate. If you live in Australia or the United States, this means your information will be transferred outside your country. We choose providers that protect information with security measures appropriate to the information they handle.

5. Who we share your information with

We share personal information only with the following, and only as described:

  • Amazon Web Services, Inc. (servers and storage): all information the App handles is stored on AWS.
  • Resend, Inc. (email delivery): your email address, to send one-time sign-in codes, password reset emails and notices.
  • Apple Inc. (Sign in with Apple and App Store): if you use Sign in with Apple, Apple confirms your identity to us. When you buy Premium, we give the App Store an internal account identifier (random letters and numbers that do not include your email address, display name or User ID) so that the purchase is linked to your account.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) (error monitoring): crash and error information, without anything that identifies you.
  • 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo) (push notifications): your push token and the content of each notification (for example, the other person’s display name, part of a comment or a place name).
  • When required by law: if disclosure is required by law, regulation or legal process, or is necessary to protect someone’s life, safety or rights.
  • If the App changes hands: if the App is transferred to another operator, your information may be transferred with it, and it will remain protected by this Policy.

Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy and its agreement with us.

6. Location in more detail

We collect location only if you explicitly allow it. If you do not, you can still post a Log by tapping a point on the map, and the map shows Logs in order of newest first instead of nearest first.

We use location only as described in Sections 1 and 2. We do not use it to track your movements or to show you ads.

“Nearby picks” notifications, if you turn them on, use your device’s location while the App is closed to let you know when you pass near a Log posted by someone you follow. This happens on your device only, and the location is not sent to our server. The feature is off by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Notifications.

7. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • When you request deletion of your account, we keep your data for 30 days so that you can restore your account. After 30 days, your account and all data linked to it in our database, including purchase information, are permanently deleted, and your photos can no longer be viewed by anyone. Copies in database backups are deleted within a further 7 days.
  • Logs and comments you delete are removed from the App straight away, and their photos can no longer be viewed by anyone.
  • If you also want the photo files themselves erased from our storage, email us (Section 15) and we will erase them.
  • Location sent when you open the map or search is not stored at all.
  • We may keep information for longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed to deal with a report, misuse or a legal claim.

8. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted photo storage, expiring signed URLs for photos, hashed passwords, and restricted access to our servers. No system is completely secure, but if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access: you can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: you can change your display name, User ID, profile photo, gender, year and month of birth, and your Logs and comments in the App, or ask us to correct other information.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account in Settings (see Section 7). Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription; cancel it in your device’s Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Location and notifications: you can turn location access and notifications on or off at any time in your device’s settings.

To make a request, email us at the address in Section 15. We may need to confirm your identity (for example, by asking you to write from the email address on your account). We will respond within 30 days, and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

10. Children

  • The App is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If you live in Australia, you must be at least 16 to have an account.
  • If we learn that we have collected information from a child below the minimum age, we will delete the account and its information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has an account, please contact us.
  • Users under 18 should use the App with the permission of a parent or guardian.

11. Additional information for Australian users

  • We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
  • Overseas disclosure: as explained in Sections 4 and 5, we disclose personal information to recipients in Japan and the United States.
  • Anonymity: you do not have to use your real name. You can choose any display name and User ID.
  • Access and correction: you can ask to access or correct your personal information as described in Section 9.
  • Complaints: if you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please email us first. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

12. Additional information for United States users

Depending on the state where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others with consumer privacy laws), you may have the rights below. We offer them to all U.S. users.

  • Categories we collect (in the past 12 months): identifiers (email address, display name, User ID, Sign in with Apple identifier, push token); commercial information (Premium purchases); precise geolocation (only as described in Section 6); photos and other content you post; and optional demographic information (gender, year and month of birth). Sources and purposes are described in Sections 1 and 2.
  • Disclosure: we disclose these categories only to the service providers in Section 5, for the business purposes described there.

  • Your rights: you can ask to know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and disclose it, to get a copy, to correct it, and to delete it. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. You can also use an authorized agent, who may need to show us your written permission.
  • Appeals: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can contact your state Attorney General.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: because we do not track you across other sites or apps and do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing for these signals to switch off.
