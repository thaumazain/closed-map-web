Frequently asked questions

Can I join without an invite?

Anyone can download the app and sign up. There are no public maps, though: you only see someone's posts once they approve your follow request, or when you connect through their invite link.

Who can see the photos I post?

Only people whose follow request you have approved and people you connected with through an invite link. Posts set to "Only me" (Premium) are visible to you alone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Delete account" at the bottom. If you sign in again within 30 days, the deletion is cancelled. After 30 days, all of your data is permanently deleted. If you subscribe to Premium, deleting your account does not stop App Store billing, so please cancel your subscription first.

An invite link won't open

Make sure the app is installed. After installing it, tap the link again and it will open in the app.

My photo won't upload

Check your network connection and try again. If it still fails, please contact us below.
